# Daily Practice Log

Working log of timed drills, study sessions, and observations. The point is honest tracking, not polish.

## Format

Each entry follows this structure:
- **Date** and day of plan
- **Drill**: problem attempted, time taken, language, where it slowed down
- **Study**: topic covered, key takeaways
- **Notes**: what felt easy, what felt hard, gaps to revisit
